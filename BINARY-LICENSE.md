# Mnemovela-open Binary Distribution Terms

The prebuilt Mnemovela binaries attached to Mnemovela-open releases are NOT covered by
the Apache-2.0 license of this repository's source content. They are built from
the Mnemovela engine and distributed under separate binary-distribution terms, to be
finalized with the engine's licensing decision. Until then they are provided
free to use, as-is, without warranty.
